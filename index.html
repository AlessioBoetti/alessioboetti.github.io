About me

From raw data to production impact

I'm an AI Engineer & Data Scientist based in Italy, specialized in building end-to-end ML pipelines that go all the way from messy raw data to deployed, monitored, production systems.

I work at the intersection of banking and fintech domain expertise and cutting-edge machine learning — from antifraud systems protecting millions of transactions, to GenAI chatbots for customer service or internal use, to forecasting business liquidity needs. However, my skills translate seamlessly across industries, making me effective in other domains, especially regulated ones, where data-driven decision-making is critical, such as insurance, IT & cybersecurity, but also e-commerce, healthcare & life sciences, pharma, legal and logistics.

I'm driven by one question: how do we make AI that actually works in the real world? Scalable, reliable, explainable, and business-aligned.
