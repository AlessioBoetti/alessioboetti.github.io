About me

From raw data to production impact

Based in Italy, I hold a Master's Degree in Artificial Intelligence. My path into the field started during my studies, when I joined the fintech sector and began building end-to-end ML pipelines — from raw data ingestion to deployed, monitored production systems. Working at the intersection of academia and industry let me continuously bridge theory and practice, each reinforcing the other.

I work at the intersection of banking and fintech domain expertise and cutting-edge machine learning — from antifraud systems protecting millions of transactions, to GenAI chatbots for customer service or internal use, to forecasting business liquidity needs through time series analysis. However, my skills translate seamlessly across industries, making me effective in other domains, especially regulated ones, where data-driven decision-making is critical, such as insurance, IT & cybersecurity, healthcare & life sciences, pharma, legal, but also e-commerce, logistics, marketing and others.
